Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Elegance

Savi Bangles was born from a love for traditional Indian jewelry and a desire to make it accessible to the modern woman. Based in the vibrant city of Bangalore, we curate stunning bangle stacks that blend heritage craftsmanship with contemporary design sensibilities.

Each stack is thoughtfully assembled — mixing metals, textures, and colors to create harmonious combinations that elevate any outfit. From festive celebrations to everyday elegance, our bangles are made for those who truly love dressing up.
